# CRM clients and organizations

## Statuses

- `PROSPECT`
- `ACTIVE`
- `INACTIVE`
- `ARCHIVED`

## Operational workflow

1. Create an organization manually or through lead conversion.
2. Search/filter list by query, status, industry or owner.
3. Open organization detail.
4. Update core fields and owner.
5. Add and edit contact persons.
6. Mark one contact as primary.
7. Add notes and tasks.
8. Review timeline.
9. Archive an organization when it should leave the active workflow.

## Related records

The organization detail shows related leads, notes, tasks and contacts. Existing document/form/job relations remain in broader CRM modules and are not merged into this PR's scope.
