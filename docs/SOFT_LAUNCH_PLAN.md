# Soft Launch Plan

Status: **BLOCKED - do not start launch while NO-GO conditions remain**

## Principles

- Start limited.
- Do not run large paid campaigns on day one.
- Use real flows with a small trusted group only after staging and legal approvals.
- Manually review first documents, payments and invoices.
- Keep launch switches reversible.

## Stages

| Stage | Entry condition | Scope | Exit condition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Internal test | All P0 closed, staging green | Internal users only | Critical flows pass and logs clean |
| Trusted users | Legal/support ready | Small selected users | No P0/P1 incidents for agreed period |
| Limited organic traffic | Checkout/document flags approved | Organic traffic only | Stable conversion/support/error metrics |
| Limited paid traffic | Marketing gate approved | Small-budget campaign | No P0/P1 production issues |
| Full marketing | 7/14/30 day stability confirmed | Scaled campaigns | Ongoing monitoring |

## Manual controls for first customers

- Manual review of first generated documents.
- Manual review of first payments.
- Manual review of first invoices.
- Manual CRM lead qualification.
- Daily support review during first week.

## Stop conditions

- Payment mismatch.
- Wrong document delivered.
- Private data access anomaly.
- Repeated generation failure.
- Support backlog exceeds owner capacity.
